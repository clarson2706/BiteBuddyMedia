# Concept 3 — "Snap. Scan. Speak. Done."

**Format:** Rapid listicle — five ~2s clips, one product.
**Why it works:** Fast cutting matches native scroll behavior and resets
attention every beat. Directly answers the #1 objection to trackers — *logging
is tedious.*

## Hook (0–2s)
> *"Five ways to log a meal in under 3 seconds each."*

On-screen text: **"5 ways. Pick your lazy."**

## 15-second beat sheet
| Time | Visual (real screen) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Quick intro / hook | "5 ways. Pick your lazy." |
| 2–4s | Photo scan (viewfinder) | "Snap it." |
| 4–6s | Barcode scanner | "Scan it." |
| 6–8s | Voice entry | "Say it." |
| 8–10s | Label scan | "Photograph the label." |
| 10–13s | Saved foods / go-tos, one-tap | "Or one-tap a favorite." |
| 13–15s | Today tab, `buddy_happy` | **"Snap. Scan. Speak. Done."** → end card |
