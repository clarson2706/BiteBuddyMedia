# Concept 4 — "I was wrong about calorie apps"

**Format:** Before/After — establish the pain, hard-cut to the clean UI.
**Why it works:** The chaos→calm contrast is the strongest visual argument for
*why switch*, and it frames BiteBuddy directly against database-search trackers
(the Cal AI-style category).

## Hook (0–2s)
Frustrated face + fast montage of typing into a search-database tracker,
scrolling endless food lists.
On-screen text: **"every other tracker:"**
VO: *"I was wrong about calorie apps being this annoying."*

## 15-second beat sheet
| Time | Visual | Audio / On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Frustration + generic tracker montage | Hook line + **"every other tracker:"** |
| 2–4s | **Hard cut** — black frame → BiteBuddy camera-snap sound | Text: **"BiteBuddy:"** |
| 4–10s | Clean screen rec: snap → food result in seconds | VO: *"Photo. Done. It even shows its work so I can trust it."* |
| 10–15s | Today tab, `buddy_balanced_glow` (zen) | Text: **"Nutrition tracking that feels like checking in on a friend."** → end card |
