# Where $20/day should go, and the number that decides it

*Written 2026-08-02 at Connor's request. This is a recommendation, not a spend. Money is
Connor's call every time, per `CLAUDE.md`. $20/day is $140/week and $460 between now and
the Aug 25 sprint deadline.*

## Start with the arithmetic, because it changes the answer

Current funnel, all measured today:

| Step | Actual |
|---|---|
| TikTok views | ~850/day and climbing |
| View to profile view | 0.2% |
| Installs | ~0.4/day (53 customers total, 3 in the last 8 days) |
| Install to paid | **1.9%** (1 subscriber from 53 customers) |
| Price | $7.99/mo, $49.99/yr, 3-day trial |

Now run any paid channel through that last row. Assume a subscriber is worth roughly
three months at $7.99 before churn, which is generous for an app with one subscriber and
no retention data. That is $24 gross, about **$20 net** after Apple's cut.

At a 1.9% install-to-paid rate, buying one subscriber means buying **53 installs**. For
that to break even, installs have to cost **under $0.38 each**.

Nothing costs $0.38. Apple Search Ads in US health and fitness runs $2 to $5 per install.
TikTok ads are cheaper per click and worse per install. The gap is not a factor of two, it
is a factor of five to thirteen.

**So the honest headline: at today's conversion rate, no paid channel works. Not at $20 a
day, not at $200.** Spending into a 1.9% install-to-paid rate converts cash into installs
that leave. The constraint is not traffic, and this week's data says that twice over,
because reach already went up 5x and revenue did not move at all.

## What I would actually do with the $20

**$0 on ads. All of it on creators, and only 3 or 4 of them.**

The creator lever is the only one with a structural multiple, and it has never been
tested. Twenty DMs were written on Jul 25 and none were sent; the offer is $0 upfront plus
30% of a first payment. That offer is the problem. A creator with 20k engaged followers is
being asked to make content for a nutrition app with 1 follower, on spec, for a share of
revenue that does not exist yet. The observed reply rate on that offer is what you would
expect: nothing.

$20/day is $140/week, which is **2 creator posts a week at $50 to $75 flat**, or 3 at $45.
That changes the conversation from "work for free on a promise" to "here is $60, one post,
keep the free Pro, keep the 30% on top." Nano-creators in this niche take that deal
routinely.

Why creator traffic and not ad traffic, specifically:

- It arrives warm. A recommendation from someone you follow converts far better than an
  interruption, which matters enormously when the paywall is the untested part.
- It compounds. A creator post keeps being served; a paused ad stops that hour.
- It is the *only* thing that produces the missing signal. You currently have zero
  comments and zero shares across ~6,900 views. One creator post generates comments, and
  comments are the top of the metric ladder you can actually read.
- It is testable at exactly this budget. Four posts is enough to see whether creator
  traffic converts differently from FYP traffic. It is not enough to see whether ads do.

**Cap it at four posts and stop.** If four paid creator posts produce fewer than about 40
installs between them, creator distribution is not the unlock either, and the answer is
unambiguously the product funnel rather than any channel.

## The thing worth more than the $20

**Get `Analytics/installs.jsonl` populated.** It costs ten seconds a week and nothing in
dollars. Right now nothing in this repo can connect a post, a creator, or an ad to an
install, which means any spend is unmeasurable by construction. Spending $460 without it
is buying a result you will not be able to read.

Second, and bigger: **1.9% install-to-paid is the number to attack.** Category median for
freemium health apps is roughly 2 to 5%, so this is low-normal rather than broken, but at
this price point it is the difference between paid acquisition being impossible and being
merely expensive. If it moves to 5%, break-even CPI goes from $0.38 to $1.00 and Apple
Search Ads on brand terms starts to look arguable. Onboarding, when the paywall appears,
and what the 3-day trial asks for are all upstream of it. That is an app change, not a
marketing change, which is why it is not in this repo, but it is the highest-leverage
thing on this list.

## If the answer has to include ads

If Connor wants a paid line running regardless, the least-wrong version is **Apple Search
Ads, brand-exact only, $5/day, capped**. Bidding on your own app name is the one buy where
intent is already maximal and CPI is lowest, it defends the term against competitors
bidding on it, and it produces a clean CPI reading in about ten days. Treat it as
instrumentation, not growth. Do not fund a discovery campaign or a TikTok Spark Ad until
install-to-paid clears 4%, because before then every incremental install is sold at a loss.

## Summary

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| TikTok Spark Ads boosting a post | **No.** Amplifies reach that already fails to convert. |
| Apple Search Ads, discovery | **No.** Break-even needs $0.38 CPI; the category charges $2 to $5. |
| Apple Search Ads, brand-exact, $5/day | Defensible as measurement only, if a paid line is wanted. |
| **Paid creator posts, $50 to $75 flat, cap at 4** | **Yes.** Tests the one lever never tested, at exactly this budget. |
| Populating `installs.jsonl` | **Free, and prerequisite to all of the above.** |
