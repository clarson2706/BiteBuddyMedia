# F3 — "I was wrong about calorie apps"

> **⚠️ UPDATED CONTENT MODEL (2026-07) — this overrides the slide plan below.**
> Posts now **educate first and sell last**: body slides teach (nutrition facts,
> real food photos, honest tips) with **no app UI and no per-slide Buddy branding**.
> The **only app slide is the last one** — the Today-home hero in a phone silhouette
> + "Download BiteBuddy — free on the App Store" + a topic call-to-action. Every
> image is a constant **1080×1350 (4:5)**. Use the angle bank + hooks below for
> ideas, but build slides per the current SKILL.md "Content model", not the old
> screenshot-heavy tables here.


**Format:** Confession / first-person story carousel. Buddy plays the emotional
narrator; the story is the viewer's own frustration, resolved by the app.
**Hook type:** Confession + identity shift (identity hooks are the single
highest-performing type in 2026 fitness content).
**Why it works:** Cold viewers are *skeptical* of calorie apps — they've quit
three. A confession hook ("I gave up on tracking… then this") disarms that
skepticism and reframes BiteBuddy as "the one that's different," which is exactly
our App Store positioning ("the one that finally sticks").

## Guardrails
"30 days straight" is a **habit/streak** claim (fine), not a weight-loss claim
(never). No before/after body imagery, no numbers on the scale. Keep it about the
*behavior* sticking. App numbers are AI estimates the user reviews.
