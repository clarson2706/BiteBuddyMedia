> **⚠️ UPDATED CONTENT MODEL (2026-07) — this overrides the slide plan below.**
> Posts now **educate first and sell last**: body slides teach (nutrition facts,
> real food photos, honest tips) with **no app UI and no per-slide Buddy branding**.
> The **only app slide is the last one** — the Today-home hero in a phone silhouette
> + "Download BiteBuddy — free on the App Store" + a topic call-to-action. Every
> image is a constant **1080×1350 (4:5)**. Use the angle bank + hooks below for
> ideas, but build slides per the current SKILL.md "Content model", not the old
> screenshot-heavy tables here.


**Format:** Pure product-proof carousel. Real app screenshots inside phone
frames walk through the magic moment: snap → analyzing → itemized result → done.
Buddy peeks over the bezel on each slide.
**Hook type:** Speed / constraint ("3 seconds, not 3 minutes").
**Why it works:** This is the "felt like magic on video" moment Cal AI built
their company on — the scan-to-macros reveal — but as a swipeable demo that works
in total silence. It's the most literal "here's exactly what you get," which
makes it both the best **evergreen how-it-works** post and the best **Spark-Ad
creative** to put paid spend behind once it's proven organically.
