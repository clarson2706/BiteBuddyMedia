# F1 — "Buddy's List" (the golf-app clone) ⭐ HERO FORMAT

> **⚠️ UPDATED CONTENT MODEL (2026-07) — this overrides the slide plan below.**
> Posts now **educate first and sell last**: body slides teach (nutrition facts,
> real food photos, honest tips) with **no app UI and no per-slide Buddy branding**.
> The **only app slide is the last one** — the Today-home hero in a phone silhouette
> + "Download BiteBuddy — free on the App Store" + a topic call-to-action. Every
> image is a constant **1080×1350 (4:5)**. Use the angle bank + hooks below for
> ideas, but build slides per the current SKILL.md "Content model", not the old
> screenshot-heavy tables here.


**Format:** Mascot-narrated listicle carousel. Buddy carries every slide; each
slide is one "mistake"; the last slide is the app with a download CTA.
**Hook type:** Mistake correction (top-3 performing in health/fitness 2026).
**Why it works:** This is exactly the golf-app format ("7 things destroying your
swing" → download). Buddy is our animator-free golfer — instant brand
recognition, and his face is the pattern-interrupt on every slide. Most ownable
and the easiest to turn into an endless **weekly series**.

> This is the master template for the whole series. Below is the flagship
> instance, then the fill-in-the-blank so you can spin a new one every week.

## Guardrails

as "bad." Frame as awareness ("easy to under-count"), not restriction. Screenshot
numbers are AI estimates the user reviews. No Meal Advisor.
