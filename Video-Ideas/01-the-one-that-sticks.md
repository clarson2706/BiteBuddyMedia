# Concept 1 — "The one that finally sticks"

**Format:** UGC talking-head confession → screen-recording proof.
**Why it works:** Confession is the highest-converting hook type for app
installs — it disarms skepticism ("every tracker gets deleted") and creates
curiosity about why *this* one didn't.

## Hook (0–1.5s)
Real person, face to camera:
> *"I've deleted every calorie app in about a week. This is the first one I didn't."*

On-screen text (in by second one): **"POV: the tracker that actually stuck"**

## 15-second beat sheet
| Time | Visual | Audio / On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Face to camera (UGC) | Hook line above + text overlay |
| 2–7s | Screen rec: Scan → camera capture → analyzing ("Buddy is thinking") → food result with itemized macros | VO: *"You just take a photo."* |
| 7–11s | Today tab, buddy `buddy_happy`, macro rings filling | VO: *"…and this little guy is genuinely happy every time I log."* |
| 11–15s | "Your Streak" screen, growing streak + XP | Text: **"Streaks that stick. Not perfection."** → end card: app icon + *"BiteBuddy — AI Calorie Scanner"* |
