# Concept 5 — "Meet your buddy"

**Format:** Mascot-led character / personality (green-screen-style).
**Why it works:** The buddy is BiteBuddy's unfair advantage — no competitor
has an emotional character. Character content is high-engagement and
opinion-driven → drives comments → algorithm reach. Most ownable & meme-able of
the five; best organic bet.

## Hook (0–2s)
The buddy `buddy_thinking` / `buddy_warning_check` pose animated big on screen.
On-screen text: **"your food has a witness now 👀"**

## 15-second beat sheet
| Time | Visual | Audio / On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Buddy inspector pose, big | **"your food has a witness now 👀"** |
| 2–6s | Log a heavy meal → `buddy_heavy_meal` (paw on belly); then a high-protein meal → `buddy_protein_powerup` (flex) | Playful captions per reaction |
| 6–11s | Log a good day → "Your Streak" + `buddy_level_up` | Text: **"It reacts to every meal. Cheers every win."** |
| 11–15s | `buddy_goal_celebration` confetti over Today rings complete | Text: **"Meet your buddy — an AI calorie counter that's genuinely happy to see you."** → end card |
