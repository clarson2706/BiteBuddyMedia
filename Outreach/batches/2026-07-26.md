## Why only 13, not 20

TikTok research this round hit a real wall after finding these 3: a follow-up pass ran
roughly 50 additional search queries across every angle available (hashtags, dish-specific
searches, university newspaper features, Reddit-style recommendation queries) and verified
**zero** new creators. The structural problem: WebFetch (direct page loading) has been
unavailable all session (confirmed on my end too, not just the subagents), so all research
runs through WebSearch's indexed snippets only. In practice that means anything discoverable
this way has usually already been covered by press or an SEO listicle, which means it's
already a mega account by the time it surfaces. Genuine small/undiscovered creators in these
niches almost certainly exist and are reachable through TikTok's own in-app search or FYP,
just not through this search-only method. Dietitian-students also came up completely empty
on both platforms today (it was covered yesterday only via professional/dietitian-adjacent
accounts, not a genuine student).

Two Instagram entries (`@losing_glori`, `@dinnerunder30lessthan30`) are slightly under the
usual 2k follower floor (~1,800 and ~1,500) but have unusually strong, specific personalization
hooks, so they're included with a flag rather than dropped outright, your call on the floor.
