# DM Playbook — creator outreach, from first message to payout

*The operating manual for the creator program. Claude writes every message; Connor
presses send. Written 2026-07-25.*

## THE DEAL (the only version we offer)

> **Free BiteBuddy Pro for 6 months, renewed for as long as we're actively working
> together, + 30% of each attributed subscriber's FIRST payment only.** Annual sub
> ($49.99) pays the creator ~$15. Monthly ($7.99) pays ~$2.40. One payment per
> subscriber, then done. No trailing commission, no retainers, nothing upfront.
> Paid monthly over PayPal once earnings pass $10 (rolls over below that). Terms:
> `Outreach/CREATOR-TERMS.md`.

**Why 6 months, not forever:** free Pro costs us real AI compute per active user
(roughly $1-4/month depending on scan volume). Six months keeps that cost bounded
per creator instead of an open-ended, ever-growing liability as the program scales
to ~100 DMs/week. "Active partnership" means they've posted about BiteBuddy at
least once since the last renewal, or a deal is still genuinely in motion (trial,
recent reply, upcoming post). Renewal is automatic and low-friction, not a
re-negotiation, unless a creator has gone fully silent (see follow-up discipline
below), in which case the free tier lapses with the relationship.

Do not improvise other structures in DMs. "Can you pay a flat fee upfront?" gets a
polite no (canned reply below). Star performers can be renegotiated later by Connor,
from a position of data.

## Hard rules for every outbound message

1. **No em dashes. Ever.** Not in DMs, replies, briefs, captions we hand creators, or
   the terms doc. Use commas, periods, or parentheses.
2. **Personalized or not sent.** Every first message references one specific, real
   thing from that creator's content (a named series, a specific post, a format they
   use). "Love your content!" is spam. If there is nothing specific to say, we picked
   the wrong creator; skip them.
3. **Never pretend to be big.** Small is the pitch: brand new app, founding partners,
   direct line to the founder. Zero-follower accounts claiming traction read as scam.
4. **No false scarcity, no fake deadlines, no "re-upping this offer."**
5. **Lowercase-casual register on TikTok, plain-professional-casual on Instagram.**
   Sound like a person typing on a phone, because one is.
6. **Volume caps: 10/day/platform, spread across the day,** never batch-fired in one
   sitting. Protects Connor's accounts from spam flags and keeps replies manageable.
7. **Follow-up discipline: one bump after 4 or 5 days, one final nudge after 2 weeks,
   then never again.** Three unanswered messages is the hard stop.
8. **Disclosure is non-negotiable** once a deal exists: their posts must carry #ad or
   the platform's paid-partnership tag. It is in the terms and we remind them in the
   onboarding pack.

## Who we target

**US-based creators only** (updated 2026-07-26; international creators are out of
scope for now), nano and micro (2k to 50k followers, tightened down from 60k), in:
macro-friendly recipes, budget meals, college food, new-lifter fitness, honest
weight-loss journeys, busy-parent meals, dietitian students. Engagement over
follower count: real comments, replies to their commenters, a consistent format.
Skip: anyone selling crash diets or detoxes, anyone whose content violates our
guardrails, mega accounts (they want retainers), and medication-focused accounts
(we serve that audience by situation, never by prescription, and partner content
must do the same). If a creator's follower count can't be confirmed, or a specific
platform's number is confirmed but their footprint elsewhere is clearly much
larger (e.g. a small Instagram but a six-figure YouTube), flag it rather than
silently including or excluding them, Connor's call.

## The first message

Structure, four beats, 60 to 120 words:

1. **Specific compliment** tied to a real post or series of theirs (proof we looked)
2. **Who we are, one line:** small AI calorie scanner, photo in, editable estimate out
3. **The offer, one line:** free Pro for 6 months (longer if we keep working
   together) plus 30% of the first payment from anyone who subscribes through
   their link
4. **Low-pressure ask:** want to try it first? (Never "let me know if interested in
   collaborating," which is template language)

## Conversation flow (what happens after they reply)

**Stage 1, they reply with any interest** → send the try-first pack:
> awesome. here's the app: [App Store link]. I set you up with Pro free for 6
> months, just tell me the email or Apple ID name you sign up with and it activates
> within a day. try it on real food for a few days and tell me what it gets wrong,
> genuinely useful for us either way.
(Claude grants the RevenueCat entitlement when Connor forwards the identifier.)

**Stage 2, they like it / ask about the deal** → send terms + link:
> here's the whole deal in one page: [terms link]. short version: you get 6 months
> of Pro free, renews as long as we're working together, plus a personal link and a
> code. anyone who subscribes through it, you get 30% of their first payment
> (that's about $15 per annual sub). paid monthly by PayPal once you're over $10.
> reply "I agree" and I'll send your link, your code, and a little starter pack.
> one rule from the lawyers of the internet: tag posts #ad or use the paid
> partnership label.

**Stage 3, they agree** → Claude generates their onboarding pack (see below), Connor
sends it. Status → `deal`.

**Stage 4, they post** → Connor reacts personally (real comment, reshare to story if
IG). Claude logs the post, tracks it in analytics with `source: creator`. Status →
`posted`.

**Stage 5, monthly** → payout DM with the math shown:
> quick payout update: your link drove [N] subscribers this month, [breakdown].
> your 30% comes to $[X], sending to your PayPal now. thanks for being early.

## Canned replies (adapt the wording, keep the substance)

- **"Do you pay upfront?"** → "we can't do upfront yet, we're too small to spend
  money we don't have. that's exactly why the rev share is generous and permanent
  on first payments. if your post performs, you get paid every month people keep
  subscribing through your link, and if we grow, the first creators are who we
  come back to with budgets."
- **"How do I know I'll get paid / how is it tracked?"** → explain the personal
  link + code plainly, offer to show the App Store campaign dashboard screenshot
  for their link. Honesty is the sell.
- **"Is this a bot/AI/spam?"** → Connor answers this one personally and specifically.
  Never a canned line. (This is also why volume caps and personalization matter:
  the goal is never triggering this question.)
- **Silence** → bump once after 4 or 5 days ("no worries if not your thing, one
  nudge and I'll leave you alone: [one new specific line about their content]").
  Final nudge at 2 weeks. Then stop forever.
- **No** → "all good, thanks for reading it. if you ever want the free Pro anyway
  just say the word." Status → `declined`, never re-contact with the same offer.

## The onboarding pack (Claude generates per creator on "I agree")

1. Their **personal App Store link** (unique `ct=` campaign parameter, their handle)
2. Their **creator code** (their handle, for captions and comments)
3. A **one-paragraph brief**: which of our personas their audience maps to, 3 hook
   ideas in their voice (suggestions, not scripts), and the guardrails in plain
   words (no medical or outcome claims, numbers are editable AI estimates, say so)
4. **Assets on request**: real app screenshots from `UI-Library/`, Buddy poses,
   b-roll of the scan flow
5. The reminder: #ad or paid-partnership label on every post

## Batch generation (how the 140/week happens) — updated 2026-07-26

**All research happens once a week, Sunday morning.** This replaced the old
twice-weekly (Sunday + Wednesday) cadence. Full procedure: see the
`creator-pipeline` skill (`.claude/skills/creator-pipeline/SKILL.md`). Summary:

- **Sunday:** research and write 140 creators total (10 Instagram + 10 TikTok x 7
  days), US-only, 2k-50k followers, split into 7 staged files:
  `Outreach/queue/YYYY-MM-DD.md` for Monday through the following Sunday. Every
  creator gets appended to `creators.jsonl` with `status: dm_written`.
- **Every day (including Sunday):** the day's queued file is delivered (email, once
  a sending connector is set up; until then, presented in the routine's own output)
  and archived to `Outreach/batches/YYYY-MM-DD.md` so there's a permanent record of
  what actually went out, distinct from what's still queued.
- Each batch entry: handle, profile link, one-line who-they-are, one-line why-fit,
  and the ready-to-copy personalized DM.
- Claude researches every profile before writing; a creator who can't be
  personalized for gets replaced, not templated at. If Sunday's research falls
  short of 140 (this has happened, TikTok in particular can be a hard search
  surface some weeks), the shortfall is documented honestly in that day's file
  rather than padded with weak or unverifiable leads.
- Connor's job: each day, copy, paste, send, spread through the day (10/day/platform
  cap still applies); forward replies back.
