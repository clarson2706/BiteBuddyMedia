# BiteBuddy Creator Partner Terms

*Version 1.0, effective 2026-07-25. Plain-language agreement between you (the
creator) and BiteBuddy (Connor Larson, sole operator). You accept these terms by
replying "I agree" to the message that linked you here. Written without em dashes
and without legalese on purpose; the plain meaning is the intended meaning.*

## 1. What you get

- **BiteBuddy Pro, free, for 6 months, renewed for as long as we're actively
  working together.** We activate it on the account you tell us about. "Actively
  working together" means you've posted about BiteBuddy at least once since your
  last renewal, or we're still mid-conversation about an upcoming post. Renewal is
  automatic on our end, not something you need to ask for. It is a courtesy, not
  payment, and either of us can end it if the partnership ends.
- **A commission: 30% of the first payment made by each subscriber attributed to
  you.** Examples at current pricing: an annual subscription ($49.99) earns you
  $15.00. A monthly subscription ($7.99) earns you $2.40. One commission per
  subscriber, on their first payment only. Renewals, upgrades, and later payments do
  not generate additional commission. Refunded or reversed payments do not count,
  and if a refund happens after we paid you, we deduct it from your next payout
  instead of asking for money back.

## 2. How attribution works (honestly)

- You get a **personal App Store link** and a **creator code**. Subscribers count as
  yours when they come through your link (tracked by Apple's campaign reporting) or
  enter or mention your code.
- Plain honesty: app-store attribution is imperfect. Where Apple's reporting gives
  us downloads from your link but cannot tie a specific subscription to it, we
  attribute using your link's downloads and the app's overall paid-conversion rate,
  rounded in your favor. When our app adds a "who sent you" field at signup, that
  becomes the primary method. We will always show you the numbers behind your
  payout, and if you believe the count is wrong, tell us and we will look at it
  together.

## 3. How and when you get paid

- Monthly, within the first week of the following month, by **PayPal** to the
  address you give us.
- **Minimum payout is $10.** Balances under $10 roll forward until they cross it.
  If the program ends with a balance under $10, we pay it out anyway.
- You are responsible for any PayPal fees on your side and for your own taxes (see
  section 7).

## 4. What we ask of you

- **Disclose the partnership on every post that features BiteBuddy**: use #ad,
  #sponsored, or the platform's paid-partnership label. This is a legal requirement
  (FTC endorsement rules), not our preference, and it protects both of us.
- **Keep content honest and safe.** Specifically: no medical claims, no promised
  outcomes (no "lose X lbs"), no crash-diet or disordered-eating framing, and if
  you show the app's numbers, it is fair game to mention they are AI estimates you
  can edit. Say what you actually think of the app, including criticism. Honest
  beats polished.
- Do not spam your link, buy engagement, or use bots or incentivized signups
  (offering people money or prizes to subscribe). Commissions from manufactured
  subscriptions will not be paid.
- You make your own content. We may offer ideas, screenshots, and assets, but
  nothing is scripted and nothing is required.

## 5. Content ownership and resharing

Your content is yours. By joining, you allow us to reshare your BiteBuddy posts on
our own accounts and website with credit to you. We will not edit your content or
use it in paid ads without asking you first.

## 6. Ending the partnership

Either of us can end this at any time, for any reason, with a message. Commissions
already earned are still paid on the normal schedule. Free Pro may be deactivated
when the partnership ends.

## 7. The relationship, legally

You are an independent creator, not an employee, agent, or representative of
BiteBuddy. You cannot make promises on our behalf. You are responsible for
reporting your own income; if your yearly earnings from us cross the IRS reporting
threshold, we will ask you for a W-9 and issue the required form. We may update
these terms with notice; changes never apply retroactively to commissions already
earned.
